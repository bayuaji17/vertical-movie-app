# Artwork login

Asset implementasi: `apps/web/src/assets/login-artwork.png`. Dibuat memakai built-in image generation, dengan `docs/design/login-light-shadcn-redesign.png` sebagai referensi. Latar transparan; elemen dekoratif memakai alt kosong. Screenshot implementasi: `login-implemented-desktop.png` dan `login-implemented-mobile.png`.

## Prompt

```text
Extract and recreate ONLY the decorative photographic artwork from the LEFT PANEL of this approved login mockup. Deliver a standalone transparent-background PNG asset for the website. Two overlapping upright portrait 9:16 film stills with rounded corners: foreground young Indonesian woman in charcoal jacket at amber city sunset, behind and to the right young Indonesian woman in white shirt near a naturally lit window. Preserve their photography, warm tones, relative poses and overlap. Include the subtle thin lime rounded rectangular frame behind them and small lime play triangle near the lower right. Crop tightly around the complete composition with a small safety margin; isolated transparent background. NO UI, NO text, NO heading, NO badges, NO gray panel, NO form, NO borders around the overall image. Foreground portrait should be fully visible, supporting portrait partially overlapped. Photorealistic, restrained shadow, crisp asset. Preserve transparent background around and between the image cards.
```
